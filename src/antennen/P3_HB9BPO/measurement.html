The antenna measurement setup is very practical. We sit at a table about 5 m away from the antenna.
A 10 m long LMR195 cable runs from the antenna to {{constants.VNA_PETER_INFO}}.
Here we can also switch over to {{antenna.template_vars_dict['h_field_tx_info']}} as transmitter.
