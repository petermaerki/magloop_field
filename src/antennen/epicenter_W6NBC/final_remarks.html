The construction with PVC pipes and applied aluminum foil is minimalist.
With a minimal amount of metal, a lot is achieved. The efficiency on 10 m is impressive.
Tuning with the inner tube section, driven by strings on the left and right, is very simple.

I would be very interested to know how such an antenna holds up over time: how quickly the thin aluminum layers degrade outdoors, and whether oxides form that increase the losses.
If anyone has such an antenna and can share long-term experience, I would be very grateful.

The creativity in homebrew antennas is refreshing.
