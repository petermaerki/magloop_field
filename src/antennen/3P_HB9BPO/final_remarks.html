This homebrew magnetic loop is a very nice DIY build.

It is lightweight, simple, and easy to transport.
The self-made capacitor with CNC-milled plates looks excellent and works flawlessly.
The coupling loop was already nearly perfectly matched from the start and was kept unchanged for all measurements.
The efficiency is not outstanding, but for the small loop diameter and simple construction it is appropriate.
There are probably many similar homebrew antennas around the world.
