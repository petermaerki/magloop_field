The antenna was suspended from the ceiling on the upper floor, about 0.8 m above the floor. Several indoor positions were tested; the position shown gave comparatively low damping. The surrounding construction (green roof with moist soil, nearby PV system, aluminum-coated underfloor heating, stainless steel canopies, and aluminum window frames) still makes this a challenging indoor RF environment.
