Magnetic Field Calculations
Magnetic loop dipole moment: $m = I_{\text{main loop}} \cdot A$.
For a circular loop: $m = I_{\text{main loop}} \cdot \pi \left(\frac{D}{2}\right)^2$.
Limitations: Calculations on this page use the retarded magnetic dipole formula. The model is valid for a small magnetic loop (D < 0.1 lambda), and fields are considered reliable mainly at distances of about one loop diameter or more from the origin. For larger loops, current is no longer constant around the circumference, so other formulas are required.
